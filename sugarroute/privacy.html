English

Sugar Route: Merge Cake ("the game") is developed by Yusuf Kaya ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

You do not need an account

The game is fully playable offline. There is no sign-up, no login and no profile. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — completed levels, stars, coins, gems, boosters, daily streak, settings and purchased items — is written to the app's own private storage on your device. We cannot read it and it is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data collected by third parties

The game includes services from Google and Apple. These companies act as independent controllers of the data they collect and process it under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhy it is usedWhat it may collect
Google AdMobShows interstitial and rewarded ads, and pays for the free version of the gameAdvertising identifier, IP address, device model and OS version, coarse location derived from IP, ad interaction events
Google Firebase AnalyticsTells us which levels players reach and where they stop, so we can balance difficultyA random app instance identifier, device model, OS version, country, in-game events (level started, level completed, store opened)
Google Firebase CrashlyticsReports crashes so we can fix themCrash stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, time of crash
Apple In-App PurchaseProcesses purchases and restores previous onesPurchase receipts. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details, address or Apple Account

Their policies: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase Privacy · Apple Privacy Policy

Tracking and your choice

On iOS, personalised advertising uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the game still works exactly the same and you still see ads — they are simply less relevant. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Buying the "No Ads" item removes interstitial advertising from the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold no account data, most requests are handled directly by Google or Apple. Write to us and we will point you to the right process or act on the request ourselves.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
